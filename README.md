# ADNS SPACE for Mac

ADNS SPACE is a one-shot sampler you play on an X-Y pad. Load a short sound, then click and drag on the pad. Left to right picks the note in your chosen key and scale, bottom to top picks the speed, and the built-in arpeggiator plays your sound in time.

Shape it with seven effects: filter, EQ, shifter, phaser-flanger, grain delay, reverb and auto pan. Six LFOs can move any knob, or the pad itself. A 64-step sequencer plays its own melodies and can change effect settings on each step. Record everything you play and export it as a WAV file.

It works on any Mac with **macOS 13 (Ventura) or later**.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
2. Drag **ADNS SPACE** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open **ADNS SPACE** from your Applications folder.

### The first time you open it

ADNS SPACE is free and isn't registered with Apple, so the first time you open it your Mac says it *"could not verify ADNS SPACE is free of malware"*. This only happens once:

1. Click **Done**. Don't click Move to Trash.
2. Open **System Settings** and go to **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to the **Security** section. Next to *"ADNS SPACE was blocked…"*, click **Open Anyway**.
4. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click **Open Anyway** again.

From then on it opens normally.

## Updating

Versions 1.2 and later tell you when a new version is out and install it themselves: click **Update**, then **Restart now**. Updates installed this way open without asking you to allow them again.

On older versions, download the newest `.dmg` from the link above and drag it into Applications, choosing **Replace**. Your saved takes are kept either way.

## Troubleshooting

- **"ADNS SPACE is damaged and can't be opened."** The download was probably interrupted. Delete the app and the `.dmg` and download it again.
- **No sound.** Check your Mac's output device and volume, then load a sample and touch the pad.
- **Something else?** Contact the person who sent you the link.
