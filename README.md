# ADNS SPACE for Mac

A one-shot sampler you play like a Korg Kaossilator. Load a short sample, drag on the XY pad and an arpeggiator plays it in key and in time, through a rack of effects, six LFOs and a 64-step sequencer. Everything you play can be recorded and exported as a WAV file.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/AntonisKol/adns-space-releases/releases/latest)**. On that page, under **Assets**, click the file ending in **`-mac.dmg`**.

It works on any Mac with **macOS 13 (Ventura) or later**, both Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) and Intel.

## Install

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file.
2. Drag **ADNS SPACE** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open **ADNS SPACE** from your Applications folder.

### The first time you open it

ADNS SPACE is free and isn't registered with Apple, so the first time you open it your Mac says it *"could not verify ADNS SPACE is free of malware"*. This only happens once:

1. Click **Done**. Don't click Move to Trash.
2. Open **System Settings** and go to **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to the **Security** section. Next to *"ADNS SPACE was blocked…"*, click **Open Anyway**.
4. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click **Open Anyway** again.

From then on it opens normally.

## Updating

Download the newest `.dmg` from the link above and drag it into Applications again, choosing **Replace**. Your saved takes are kept.

## Troubleshooting

- **"ADNS SPACE is damaged and can't be opened."** The download was probably interrupted. Delete the app and the `.dmg` and download it again.
- **No sound.** Check your Mac's output device and volume, then load a sample and touch the pad.
- **Something else?** Contact the person who sent you the link.
