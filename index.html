ADNS SPACE

Sampler · arp · sequencer

A one-shot sampler you play on an X-Y pad. Load a short sound, then click and drag: left to right picks the note in your key and scale, bottom to top picks the speed, and the arpeggiator plays it in time.

Download for Mac Free · macOS 13 or later

X-Y pad

Pitch on one axis, arp speed on the other. Hold keeps it playing when you let go.

Seven effects

Filter, EQ, shifter, phaser-flanger, grain delay, reverb and auto pan.

Six LFOs

Map any knob, or the pad itself, and let it move on its own.

64-step sequencer

A note on every step, and effect settings that change step by step.

Record & export

Every take is saved with its moves. Export 24-bit WAV or re-render with a new sound.

Install

ADNS SPACE is free and isn't registered with Apple, so your Mac asks you to allow it the first time.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag ADNS SPACE onto Applications.
  2. Open ADNS SPACE. When your Mac says it can't verify the app, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to ADNS SPACE.
  4. Enter your Mac password if asked. From then on it opens normally.

To update, download the new version and replace the app in Applications. Your saved takes are kept.
