Privacy

This page explains what personal data this site handles, why, who else sees it and how long it stays. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking across other sites, and nothing is sold.

Who is responsible

Antonios Kolovos, [YOUR POSTAL ADDRESS]. Questions about this page: contact[at]adnsaudio.com.

Visiting any page

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, USA) serves the site, so it receives your network address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Google Fonts (Google, USA) serves the site's fonts, so Google receives your network address.
  • Cloudflare (USA) answers the lookups for this domain's address.

The ADNS SPACE page asks GitHub for the list of releases from your browser, and downloads come from GitHub too. The Techno Vinyls app asks Discogs (USA) for records from your browser, so Discogs receives your network address, and it keeps copies of Discogs's answers in your browser's own storage for up to a day, so pages load faster. Those copies hold nothing about you.

Emailing us

Mail to adnsaudio.com addresses is forwarded by Cloudflare to a private inbox. We keep your message and address for as long as needed to answer it.

Market Trends: asking a question

  • Your question goes to Anthropic (USA), whose Claude writes the answer. Your email address and your network address are not sent with it. We don't store the question.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (USA) checks that you are a person, and sees your network address and browser details. Signed-in visitors skip this check when asking a question.
  • We count your questions to apply the limits. For that, and in our logs, your network address is kept only as a scrambled version made with a secret key, never the address itself. The logs also hold the page requested, the time and the browser name.

Question counts are deleted after two days. Logs have a fixed size limit, and the oldest entries are deleted as new ones arrive, so how long an entry stays depends on how busy the site is.

Market Trends: having an account

An account is optional and gives you more questions. For it we store:

  • your email address
  • a scrambled copy of your password that can't be turned back into it
  • when the account was created and confirmed, and when you last signed in
  • scrambled copies of your active sign-in sessions and of the links we email you

Resend (USA) sends our emails, so it receives your email address and the message. We only send what signing up needs: the confirmation link, a new-password link when you ask for one, and a notice if someone tries to sign up with an address that already has an account. There is no newsletter.

Deleting it. "Delete my account" on the Market Trends page removes all of this at once. An account that is never confirmed is deleted after 7 days. Copies remain in our backups for up to 14 days, and are then deleted.

Cookies

This site only uses cookies on Market Trends, and only those that signing in needs: mt_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and mt_confirm protects the confirmation and new-password pages for as long as their links work. Cloudflare Turnstile may also store technical information in your browser for its check.
