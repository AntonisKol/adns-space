ADNS Sample Map

Thousands of one-shots in folders called Kick_034.wav, and finding the right one means clicking through them one by one. Sample Map puts them all on one map instead.

  • Open any folder of one-shots, or your DAW's whole sample library, subfolders and packs included.
  • Every sound is analysed for brightness, noisiness, attack, decay and length, then grouped by kind: kicks, snares, hats, synths and more.
  • Hover to hear, then drag a dot straight into your DAW.
